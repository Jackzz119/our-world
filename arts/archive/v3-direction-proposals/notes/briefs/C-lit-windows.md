

# CONCEPT C · 「灯火小楼」Lit Windows

## Hypothesis

Everyone you love lives one lit window away. The world is a cozy hand-painted building shown as a dollhouse cutaway. Each person owns a room; a lit window means they are online; the camera dollies from the whole building into any room. Couples share connected rooms with a small door or round window in the shared wall (knock on the wall, slide a note under the door). Shared spaces for groups: a rooftop garden, a ground-floor café kitchen, a lobby with mailboxes. Your own room is home base: decorate it and play in it. Companion moment: when you zoom into someone's room, their avatar looks up at the camera and waves.

Presence translation: each room shows its owner's real state (gaming, painting, cooking, asleep with lights dimmed); when the owner is offline the window goes dark but the building cat sits on the sill.

## Style

Stylized, chibi-leaning hand-drawn humans (about 3.5 heads tall), rounded shapes, big expressive but simple eyes, colored-pencil plus gouache textures, picture-book warmth; diorama rooms full of lived-in props; wet night streets with reflections; strong contrast between warm interior light and blue rain outside. Scalable: many characters on screen, cheap to customize and animate.

## Scale, identity, play

- Multi-person is native: every friend is a neighbor; groups meet on the rooftop or in the café.
- Customization: avatar maker plus room decoration (furniture catalog, drag and place): monetizable and international-friendly.
- Scene gameplay (never duplicates UI): rooftop garden: each friend has a planter, you can water other people's plants, blooms and harvests become gifts and memories; café kitchen: a latte-art mini-game (pour milk, draw a heart), then deliver the cup to someone's door and it appears in their room; knock on the wall: tap the shared wall → the partner's avatar knocks back and little hearts pop; lobby mailboxes: letters, polaroids and voice-note cassettes left for friends; a building cat wanders between rooms, pet it for a purr.
