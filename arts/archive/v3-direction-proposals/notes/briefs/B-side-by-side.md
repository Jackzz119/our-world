

# CONCEPT B · 「并肩」Side by Side

## Hypothesis

Companionship without performance: "the same view, the same song, shoulder to shoulder." Both avatars sit together, seen from behind or over the shoulder, facing a huge living view (a window seat in a high-rise, a balcony, a rooftop, a night train). The viewer sees themself AND their partner, so it reads as "us" watching the world rather than two strangers talking. The view is the Wallpaper-Engine-grade hero: a deep multi-plane parallax cityscape with drifting clouds, trains, traffic light trails, blinking aircraft, windows switching on at dusk, rain, fireworks, seasons. Faces are a reward: tap your partner and they turn around toward the camera and smile while their recorded voice line plays.

Presence translation: both online → shoulders close, sometimes leaning; partner listening to music → one earbud each (shared earbuds); away → an empty cushion with a folded blanket and a warm cup; asleep → curled under a blanket on the window seat.

## Style

Painterly hand-drawn illustration: gouache and watercolor textures, visible brush strokes, soft simplified faces with small features, natural proportions (about 6.5 heads tall), atmospheric perspective, cinematic sunset and night light; the feel of a hand-painted animated feature film background with the characters integrated into it. Characters are small-to-medium in frame and mostly seen from behind or in three-quarter back view, so silhouettes carry identity (hair shape, outfit, scarf, headphones); this makes customization cheap and forgiving (show it).

## Scale, identity, play

- Multi-person: the bench gets longer. Friends sit in a row on a rooftop ledge, a hill bench or riverside steps (scales linearly from 2 to 8). An offline friend's spot keeps a blanket and a can of soda.
- Customization: a paper-doll creator with a front / back toggle (the back view is what the scene shows most), silhouette-first choices: hairstyle, hair color, outfits, scarves, hats, headphones; plus 坐姿 (sitting pose: hugging knees, cross-legged, leaning back) and a voice card.
- Scene gameplay (never duplicates UI): balcony garden: plant seeds together, plants grow in real time over days, both partners have their own watering can, the first bloom becomes a memory card; share earbuds: drag one earbud onto your partner → shared listening, both heads bob, city lights twinkle to the beat; paper-plane letters: fold a short note into a paper plane and throw it into the view, it glides across the city and lands at the partner; anniversary sky lanterns / fireworks: tap a lantern on the sill to release it, on anniversaries fireworks bloom over the city; feed the stray cat that visits the window ledge.
