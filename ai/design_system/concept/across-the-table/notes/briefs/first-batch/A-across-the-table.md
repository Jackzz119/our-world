

# CONCEPT A · 「对坐」Across the Table

## Hypothesis

The answer to "one character or two" is the camera, not the head-count. The camera IS the viewer. Each person sees their partner's avatar sitting across a small table, facing them, so everyone gets "someone sitting in front of me" and that someone is a real person: 小满 sees 阿屿 across the table on her screen, 阿屿 sees 小满 on his. The viewer's own presence is implied by first-person foreground props (their own mug and hands, phone, notebook) and by their reflection in the night window glass.

Presence translation (the avatar mirrors the real person): partner typing → texting on a phone; listening to music → over-ear headphones, head gently bobbing; browsing memories → flipping a photo album; away → empty chair with their jacket and a still-steaming cup; asleep at night → head resting on folded arms on the table. Now and then the avatar glances up at the camera with a soft smile: eye contact is the emotional core.

## Style

High-end 2D anime CG illustration at gacha key-visual quality: clean fine line art, soft cel shading with painterly gradients, warm subsurface skin, detailed hair highlights, semi-realistic anime proportions (not chibi, not photoreal). Cinematic lighting: a warm practical lamp as key light, cool window / rain / neon rim light, bloom, bokeh, shallow depth of field. Upper-body framing: the table hides the lower body, so only a half-body Live2D-style rig is needed (a production win worth showing).

## Scale, identity, play

- Multi-person: the table grows. 3 to 5 friends at a round table or a winter kotatsu, seen in first person; seats at 10, 12 and 2 o'clock; an offline friend's seat keeps a trace (folded scarf, cup, a tangerine).
- Customization: an anime avatar maker on one shared half-body template rig: face base, eyes, brows, hair front / back / side (with physics), hair color gradient, skin tone, tops, accessories; plus a 声音名片 voice card: record 3 to 5 short lines (greeting, poke reaction, good night) that play when the partner taps your avatar.
- Scene gameplay (never duplicates UI): tap or pat the avatar → a reaction animation plus their recorded voice line (waveform bubble); pour-over coffee ritual: hold to pour from a gooseneck kettle, steam rises, the cup slides across the table and appears on the partner's side on their screen, the avatar takes a sip; fogged-window doodle: draw with a finger on steamed glass, it stays on the partner's window until they wipe it; vinyl: drop the needle → shared listening, both wear headphones, the lamp light breathes gently with the music; instant camera: snap the table (both reflections in the glass) → a polaroid slides into the memory wall.
