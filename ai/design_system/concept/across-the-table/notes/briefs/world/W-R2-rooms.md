

# THIS RUN · Rooms II (5 rooms × 2 images = 10 images)

Each room is one table in its own art-directed place, with a function it serves and a small game. For every room deliver two landscape images: a key art (no UI, no text) and a gameplay moment (first-person hands doing the room's ritual, the partner reacting; no UI). Alternate who sits across: when 阿屿 sits across, the viewer is 小满 (cream knit sleeves); when 小满 sits across, the viewer is 阿屿 (charcoal hoodie sleeves).

## 6. 唱片角 · 黄昏霓虹 — function: music
- Across the listening counter: 阿屿 (viewer 小满). Light: dusk through a shop window (violet and orange sky), a pink neon sign shaped like a vinyl record (no lettering), a warm lamp. Palette: dusk violet, neon pink, amber, walnut.
- Props: crates of vinyl, a turntable on the counter, two pairs of headphones, album sleeves with abstract art only, a handwritten sticky note on one sleeve.
- Game 「为你选一张」: flip through the crate, pick a record for your partner and write a short note on the sleeve; dropping the needle starts shared listening, both wear headphones and the room lights breathe gently with the beat; the dedication joins the music history.
- R-records-keyart.png: 阿屿 across holding up an album sleeve with a sticky note toward you, headphones around his neck, neon glow on his glasses.
- R-records-play.png: 小满's fingers flipping through a crate, one record pulled halfway out; across, 阿屿 lowers the needle onto the spinning record; both have headphones.

## 7. 夜行列车 · 雪原月夜 — function: long distance / time zones + postcards
- Across a small dining-car table: 小满 (viewer 阿屿). Light: moonlit snowy plains streaming past the window with motion blur, distant village lights, a warm little table lamp with a fringed shade, soft reflections on the glass. Palette: moon blue, snow white, lamp amber, burgundy velvet.
- Props: two bento boxes, tea in glasses with metal holders, a stack of postcards and a station stamp, a paper ticket, a folded train map.
- Game 「一起去远方」: the train travels while both are online; the window follows real time of day and, for long-distance couples, can show the partner's city sky; each stop adds a stamped postcard to the memory box; share a bento.
- R-train-keyart.png: 小满 across, forehead near the window watching the snowfield, turning back to you with a small smile; moon and passing lights.
- R-train-play.png: 阿屿's hand pressing a station stamp onto a postcard; 小满 across holds up her own stamped postcard; blurred station lights streaming past.

## 8. 天台 · 夏夜萤火 — function: wish list + anniversaries
- Across a low picnic table on a rooftop: 阿屿 (viewer 小满). Light: deep blue summer night, stars, a distant city glow, string lights, fireflies drifting, a small lantern on the table. Palette: indigo, firefly yellow-green, warm string-light gold.
- Props: watermelon slices, two sparklers, a portable speaker, a picnic blanket, a paper wish card and pen, a small telescope.
- Game 「许个愿」: when a shooting star crosses (a few times a night), tap it and write a wish card, which joins the shared wish list; bring your sparkler to theirs to light it (both online); on anniversaries fireworks bloom over the city.
- R-rooftop-keyart.png: 阿屿 across the low table with a lit sparkler, looking at you; fireflies, stars, string lights.
- R-rooftop-play.png: 小满's hand bringing an unlit sparkler to touch 阿屿's lit one across the table, sparks jumping over; a shooting star streaks across the sky; a wish card lies on the table.

## 9. 海边小屋 · 日出阳台 — function: alarm / morning check-in
- Across a small breakfast table on a balcony: 小满 (viewer 阿屿). Light: sunrise in pastel peach and blue, sun glitter on the sea, sheer curtains blowing, long soft shadows. Palette: peach, aqua, sand, white.
- Props: toast and jam, juice glasses, a boiled egg, a seashell wind-chime, a small calendar card, a bowl of seashells, a straw hat.
- Game 「早安」: the first person awake rings the seashell wind-chime, which gently wakes the partner's scene and plays their recorded good-morning line; make breakfast for each other (choose a topping, it is served across); pick up one seashell each morning so the bowl fills with days together.
- R-seaside-keyart.png: 小满 across the breakfast table, hair lifted by the sea breeze, holding a juice glass, squinting happily into the sunrise.
- R-seaside-play.png: 阿屿's fingers tapping the seashell wind-chime; 小满 across waking with a stretch and a yawn beside a small voice-waveform bubble; toast on the table.

## 10. 雪夜小木屋 · 壁炉 — function: ambient ASMR + a cozy ritual
- Across a low table by the fireplace: 阿屿 (viewer 小满). Light: flickering firelight as warm key, a blizzard beyond a frosted window (cool blue), fairy lights, deep cozy shadows. Palette: ember orange, pine green, snow blue, plaid red.
- Props: a plaid blanket, two cocoa mugs with marshmallows, roasting sticks, a record player, knitted socks drying by the fire, a board game box.
- Game 「烤棉花糖」: hold to toast a marshmallow at the fire (it only gets more golden, it never burns) and hand it across; drag the blanket to share it; the fire crackle is an ambient sound layer.
- R-cabin-keyart.png: 阿屿 across by the fireplace wrapped in a plaid blanket, cocoa mug in both hands, firelight on his face, snow outside.
- R-cabin-play.png: 小满's hand holding a roasting stick with a golden marshmallow near the fire; across, 阿屿 holds out his mug to receive it; embers drifting.
