

# THIS RUN · Rooms I (5 rooms × 2 images = 10 images)

Each room is one table in its own art-directed place, with a function it serves and a small game. For every room deliver two landscape images: a key art (no UI, no text) and a gameplay moment (first-person hands doing the room's ritual, the partner reacting; no UI, captions not needed). Alternate who sits across: when 阿屿 sits across, the viewer is 小满 (cream knit sleeves); when 小满 sits across, the viewer is 阿屿 (charcoal hoodie sleeves).

## 1. 街角咖啡馆 · 雨天午后 — function: companion chat + gifting
- Across: 阿屿 (viewer 小满). Light and weather: soft grey rainy daylight from a big street window, warm brass pendant lamps inside, condensation on the glass, a wet street outside with colourful umbrellas and a blurred passing tram. Palette: cocoa brown, brass, rain blue-grey, cream.
- Props: small marble table, a slice of strawberry shortcake with two forks, a chalkboard menu, a gleaming espresso machine in background bokeh, a little potted plant.
- Game 「为对方点一杯」: choose a drink for your partner and pour the latte art yourself; the cup slides across and appears on their side; the cup design joins a "cup gallery" memory.
- R-cafe-keyart.png: 阿屿 across, both hands around a cup, looking up at you with a warm smile, rain behind him.
- R-cafe-play.png: close first-person view of 小满's hands pouring steamed milk from a small pitcher into an espresso, a heart forming in the crema; 阿屿 across, softly out of focus, leaning in, delighted.

## 2. 玻璃温室 · 晴朗上午 — function: growing together + anniversary
- Across: 小满 (viewer 阿屿). Light: bright late-morning sun through a glass roof, strong volumetric light shafts, dappled leaf shadows moving over the table and her face, floating pollen and dust motes, a few raindrops left on the glass from an earlier shower. Palette: fresh greens, sunlit gold, white glass frames, terracotta.
- Props: wooden potting table, two terracotta pots with tiny wooden name tags (小满 / 阿屿), seed packets, a small copper watering can, a plant journal with pressed flowers, hanging ferns.
- Game 「一起养一盆花」: plant a seed together, each person waters when they visit, the plant grows over real days (sprout, bud, bloom); the bloom can be pressed into the shared diary; an anniversary flower opens on the anniversary date. No wilting.
- R-greenhouse-keyart.png: 小满 across the potting table, a little soil on her fingertips, cheek on her hand, light shafts falling between you, smiling at you.
- R-greenhouse-play.png: 阿屿's hands watering a small sprout in the pot tagged with both names while 小满 gently touches the new bud; a small wooden tag reads 第12天; one tiny sparkle on the new leaf.

## 3. 深夜书房 · 雪夜共读 — function: timeline diary + focusing together
- Across: 阿屿 (viewer 小满). Light: deep night, a green-glass banker's lamp pooling warm light on a wide wooden desk, snow falling past a tall window, cool moonlight on the snow, the rest of the room in soft darkness. Palette: deep navy, lamp gold, bottle green, parchment.
- Props: stacks of books, his laptop and notebook, her open diary with a fountain pen, an hourglass, a glass jar with a few folded paper stars, two cocoa mugs, a knitted blanket on the chair.
- Game 「一起专注」: either person flips the hourglass to start a shared focus session; both avatars study; when the sand runs out both stretch and a folded paper star drops into the shared jar (the jar fills over months); folded notes can be slid across; writing in the diary with the fountain pen creates a timeline entry.
- R-study-keyart.png: 阿屿 across, faint lamp reflection on his glasses, pen in hand, glancing up from his notebook at you with a quiet smile, snow outside.
- R-study-play.png: 小满's hand flipping the hourglass, sand beginning to fall; 阿屿 across nods and opens his book; at the side a new paper star drops into the star jar.

## 4. 暗房 · 红灯冲洗 — function: photo wall (shared photos)
- Across: 小满 (viewer 阿屿). Light: dramatic red safelight as key, a thin strip of cool light under the door, deep shadows, wet reflective trays. Palette: crimson, black, warm skin highlights, a touch of teal.
- Props: narrow darkroom counter, three developing trays, tongs, an enlarger, a clothesline of drying photos overhead (small memories of the two of them: a beach, a café, a ferris wheel), a timer, a small radio.
- Game 「冲洗我们的照片」: photos uploaded to the shared album arrive as blank paper; rocking the tray makes the real image slowly appear; clip it to the line; dried photos move to the photo wall. Both can develop the same roll together.
- R-darkroom-keyart.png: 小满 across under red light, holding a freshly developed photo with tongs, grinning at you over it; photos drying overhead.
- R-darkroom-play.png: 阿屿's hands rocking a tray where a photo of the two of them at a ferris wheel is half emerged; 小满 leans in to look, excited; the line of dried photos above.

## 5. 棋牌室 · 雨夜桌游 — function: mini-games + friends room (group)
- Group: 小满 across, 圆圆 on the left, 老K on the right (viewer 阿屿). Light: a low warm pendant lamp over a green felt card table, the room dim, rain on a window behind with soft neon reflections. Palette: felt green, amber, dark wood, neon pink and blue accents.
- Props: playing cards, a Gomoku board with black and white stones, dice, chips and soda cans, a doodled score pad (doodles only, no ranking), a cat asleep on a spare chair.
- Game 「桌游之夜」: light turn-based games played for real across devices (Gomoku 1v1, drawing lots for the group, rock-paper-scissors); the winning moment becomes a polaroid in memories; the winner picks the next song; no ranking.
- R-gameroom-keyart.png (group): 小满 across fanning her cards and peeking at you over them, 圆圆 laughing on the left, 老K mid-shuffle on the right, the viewer's own hand of cards in the foreground.
- R-gameroom-play.png (1v1 close): 阿屿's hand placing a black stone on the Gomoku board; 小满 across biting her lip in thought, a white stone between her fingers; lamp overhead.
