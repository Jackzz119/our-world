

# THIS RUN · Light and weather at the home table (10 images)

Show how alive the same place feels across time, season and weather. Every image is the home table from A1 (the small table by a big city window, lamp on the left, record player on the right, the viewer 阿屿's charcoal sleeves and mug in the foreground) with 小满 across. Keep the camera, table, window and furniture layout the same in all ten so they read as one scene under different conditions; 小满's pose, expression and outfit change to suit each condition. Full-size landscape images, no UI, no text. Push the lighting hard: each condition must have a clearly different key light direction, colour temperature and contrast, and visible atmospheric effects.

1. LW-01-dawn.png — 清晨: pink-gold dawn, the low sun just above the city, soft light rays, steam curling from the mugs; 小满 sleepy, hair a little messy, in a loose sweater.
2. LW-02-overcast.png — 阴天午后: soft diffused grey light, low contrast, calm; 小满 reading a paperback, glancing up.
3. LW-03-golden.png — 黄昏金光: strong golden hour, long light shafts across the table with dust motes, rich rim light on her hair; 小满 laughing.
4. LW-04-bluehour.png — 蓝调时刻: deep blue dusk, city windows switching on one by one, the lamp just turned on, her face split between cool blue and warm lamp light.
5. LW-05-storm.png — 雷雨夜: heavy rain lashing the glass, a lightning flash bleaching the room cold white for an instant, 小满 startled then smiling, both hands around her mug.
6. LW-06-snow.png — 雪夜: snow falling, frost creeping along the glass edges, warm lamp, 小满 in a scarf and knit sweater.
7. LW-07-spring.png — 春日樱花: the window open, cherry blossom petals drifting in and landing on the table and in her hair, fresh morning light, her hair lifted by the breeze.
8. LW-08-summer.png — 夏夜萤火: the window open on a summer night, fireflies drifting in, a small electric fan, an iced drink with condensation, 小满 in a light summer top.
9. LW-09-autumn.png — 秋日午后: warm amber afternoon, falling leaves outside and a few on the windowsill, 小满 in a knit sweater with a cup of tea.
10. LW-10-candle.png — 停电烛光: a power cut, only candles on the table, the city dark outside in the rain, deep warm intimate darkness, 小满's face lit from below by candlelight, smiling.
