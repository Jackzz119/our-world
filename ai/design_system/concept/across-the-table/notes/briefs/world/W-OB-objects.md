

# THIS RUN · Interactive objects on the table (10 images)

Close, tactile moments with objects that live on or around the table. Each image shows the viewer's own hands doing something, the object responding, and the partner across reacting or receiving a result. Landscape, no UI; only images marked "2-panel" or "storyboard" use short captions. Alternate partners as noted (viewer sleeves: 阿屿 = charcoal hoodie, 小满 = cream knit cardigan).

1. OB-snowglobe.png — 雪花球: at the home table at night, the viewer (阿屿) shakes a snow globe holding a tiny diorama of the café where the two of them first met; snow swirls inside and catches the lamp light; 小满 across watches with her chin on her hands. Result: shaking it replays a short "first-meeting" memory card.
2. OB-starjar.png — 星星罐许愿 (wish list): the viewer (小满) folds a paper star with a wish written on it and drops it into a glass jar full of softly glowing paper stars; 阿屿 across holds his own folded star, about to add it.
3. OB-letter.png — 蜡封信: the viewer (小满) presses a wax seal with a small star emblem onto an envelope, a short handwritten letter beside it; 阿屿 across reaches out to receive it. Result: the letter waits on his side of the table until he opens it, even if he is offline.
4. OB-lamp.png — 台灯拉绳, 2-panel with captions 关灯 / 开灯: the same home table with 小满 across; in the left panel the room is cool dark blue with only the city glow; in the right panel the viewer (阿屿) has pulled the lamp chain and warm lamplight floods the table and her face. This shows that lights in the scene are real and interactive.
5. OB-musicbox.png — 八音盒: the viewer (阿屿) winds a small wooden music box; a tiny brass couple figure turns on top, faint musical notes drift; 小满 across hums along with her eyes closed.
6. OB-calendar.png — 翻页日历: the viewer (小满) flips a wooden desk calendar to a page marked with a small heart (anniversary); paper petals and a little confetti burst from it; 阿屿 across claps and grins.
7. OB-cat.png — 家里的猫: a fluffy grey-and-white cat walks across the table between the two, rubbing its head against 小满's hand; the viewer's (阿屿's) hand reaches to pet its back; small curls suggest purring.
8. OB-polaroid.png — 拍立得, 2-panel with captions 拍下 / 显影: left, the viewer (阿屿) holds an instant camera up toward 小满, who makes a peace sign; right, the printed photo in the viewer's hand is slowly developing, showing that moment. Result: it joins the photo wall.
9. OB-radio.png — 老收音机: the viewer (小满) turns the tuning knob of a vintage radio on the table; the scene's ambient sound station changes (rain, café, forest, sea), shown by a subtle change of the window view; 阿屿 across tilts his head listening.
10. OB-phone.png — 转盘电话语音: a vintage rotary phone on the table rings (tiny motion lines) because 小满 left a voice message; the viewer (阿屿) lifts the receiver to listen; across, 小满 has her chin on her hand, smiling shyly at being heard. Result: voice messages live in the room as calls, not only in a chat list.
